Our Story

Sprout & Crumb started at my kitchen table with a bag of flour, a dog-eared notebook, and a stubborn belief that plant-based baking could taste every bit as good as the real thing.

After years of experimenting — and plenty of flat loaves along the way — I found that the secret isn't substitution. It's understanding what each ingredient does and letting plants do it better. Every item on our menu is baked fresh, without any animal products, and without any compromise on flavour.

We're a small bakery and proud of it. We know our regulars by name, we change our specials with the seasons, and we mean it when we say everything is made with care.
