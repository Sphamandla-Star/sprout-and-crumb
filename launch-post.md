## Talk Outline: "Why Plant-Based Baking is a Genuine Business Opportunity for Local Cafes"

A 5-slide presentation for café owners and buyers.

---

### Slide 1 — Title Slide

**Title:** *From Niche to Necessary: How Plant-Based Baking is Reshaping the Café Market*

**Bullet points:**
- Who we are: Sprout & Crumb, a fully plant-based bakery baking to order
- Why we're talking to you today: mutual opportunity, not a sales pitch
- What you'll walk away with: a clear picture of demand, a simple supply model, and a tasting

**Visual idea:** A close-up, warm-toned photo of a freshly baked seeded sourdough on a wooden board. Clean, confident, appetite-first.

---

### Slide 2 — The Demand Is Already in Your Café

**Title:** *Your Customers Are Already Asking*

**Bullet points:**
- 1 in 4 UK consumers now actively reduces animal products (The Vegan Society, 2023)
- "Vegan option?" is the most common dietary question at café counters
- Cafes that added plant-based baked goods reported an average 18% uplift in pastry sales (Plant Based News, 2023)

**Visual idea:** A simple split image — left side shows a café cabinet with one token "vegan" label on a single muffin; right side shows a full, abundant display of plant-based bakes. Let the contrast do the work.

---

### Slide 3 — What We Offer Cafes

**Title:** *Baked to Order. Delivered Ready to Sell.*

**Bullet points:**
- Weekly pre-order model: you order on Monday, we deliver Tuesday–Wednesday
- No wastage risk — you only pay for what you've already ordered
- Every item is labelled with allergen and dietary information, ready for your display

**Visual idea:** A flat-lay of kraft paper bags, each labelled with a product name and dietary icons (V, GF, NF). Feels like a real wholesale delivery — clean and professional.

---

### Slide 4 — Real Products, Real Margins

**Title:** *Items That Sell and Margins That Work*

**Bullet points:**
- Our best café performers: Almond Croissant (£3.20 wholesale → £4.50–£5.00 retail), Cinnamon Scroll, Seeded Sourdough by the slice
- Wholesale pricing available for orders of 6+ units per item per week
- Custom branding options for cafes that want items to feel house-made

**Visual idea:** A simple two-column table slide — left column: product name and wholesale unit price; right column: suggested retail price and margin percentage. Keep it plain and honest — numbers build trust.

---

### Slide 5 — Next Steps

**Title:** *Let's Bake Something Together*

**Bullet points:**
- Try before you commit: take a tasting box home today, on us
- Starter wholesale agreement: no minimum contract, cancel any week
- Get in touch: [your email] or visit sproutandcrumb.co.uk to pre-order and see the full menu

**Visual idea:** A warm, informal photo of two people sharing pastries over a café table with coffee. End on a human note — this is a relationship, not a transaction.
